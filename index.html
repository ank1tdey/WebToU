09 / QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before we
get started.

A few practical details about working with the studio.

What happens after I contact WebToU?+

We review your enquiry, ask for any missing project context and arrange a discussion if needed. Once the scope is clear, we share a proposal with deliverables, timeline and pricing.

How long does a website take?+

Timing depends on page count, functionality, content readiness and feedback. We confirm a realistic schedule in the proposal before starting.

Do I need to provide website content and images?+

Ideally, yes. We can guide the content structure and discuss copy or asset support as a separate part of the scope.

Can I update my website after launch?+

WordPress projects can be configured for client-side content editing. For other builds, update arrangements depend on the technology and maintenance scope agreed in advance.

Are domain and hosting included?+

They are quoted separately unless a proposal explicitly says otherwise. Third-party subscriptions, premium plugins and transaction fees are also separate.

Do you manage social media advertising?+

Paid campaign setup and management can be included in a social media scope. The advertising budget is paid separately by the client to the platform.
